## 1. What we test and why it matters

If every agent (Scientist, Extractor, Tester and the web-search summariser W) runs on the same model, how small can that model be before Rogue One stops completing its loop? We run frozen Full-v2 unchanged on five Qwen3.5 rungs (0.8B–122B-A10B) plus gpt-oss-20b and fit the share of functionally successful runs against log active parameters (C5a). At each rung we also ask whether the full loop (F2) beats simply asking the same model for feature lists many times and keeping the best by inner CV (F0-best-of-k). This is the paper's only whole-system capability evidence and sets the frame for CAP-2 (A19), RP1 (A18) and CAP-3 (A20).

**Why weak models are slow, and what keeps them alive.**
- Caps: Scientist up to 100 retries (`sci/agent.py:519`) × 200 turns (`config.py:411`); Extractor R rounds (`ext/agent.py:967`) × 100 turns (`config.py:373`); Tester 20 attempts (`tst/agent.py:1373`). Hence the 6 h cap.

**Metrics** (R13; unit in brackets).
- **`func_success`** (C5a outcome) [run] = `success` **and** the inner-CV-selected iteration contains ≥ 1 accepted, non-fallback LLM feature (01 §4.6). Because selection is per fold, A17 pre-registers the run-level reading (A17-T1): **the condition must hold in ≥ 3 of the 5 folds** (proposed default; sensitivities 5/5 and ≥ 1/5), evaluated per fold by joining E3's per-iteration active-feature log to the selected trial in `fold_aggregation_best_iterations.csv`. *Accepted* = appended by the Extractor and `Status` ≠ `Pruned` at that iteration (E3 active-feature log); *fallback* = E3's fallback flag, or, if the flag is missing, an argumentation starting with "Fallback" (`ext/agent.py:644`, `:680`, `:715`, `:790`, `:825`); raw columns and E6's augment-seeded rows (E6's seed marker in the log) never count, because under augment a run that adds nothing still passes `success`, and this metric exists to catch exactly that. A run without `fold_aggregation_summary.csv` has `func_success = 0` (ITT).
- **`success`** [run] = the run finishes within the 6 h cap with `fold_aggregation_summary.csv`, and its `heldout_score` (mean over the 5 folds at the selected iterations) is no worse than `base_no_fe` by more than 2 × fold SD: classification (balanced accuracy) `heldout_score` ≥ `base_no_fe` − 2·SD; regression (nRMSE, lower is better) `heldout_score` ≤ `base_no_fe` + 2·SD. On junglechess, additionally `heldout_score` ≥ 0.95 (the rule is found). `base_no_fe` and fold SD (population SD over the 5 outer folds, ddof 0), seed-matched, come from A05-T6's artifact `base-no-fe-ref` (`accw_balanced` and `accw_balanced_sd` for classification, `nrmse` and `nrmse_sd` for regression; the SYN-P cells are A08's CPU-screen cells), the same references A05 uses for its own `success`, so A05 and A17 rows are scored identically. The 2·SD band is wide on heart and myocardial, so real-4 `success` is a no-collapse check, not a quality claim.
- `rec_sel` / `rec_any` [fold-episode]: an active non-fallback feature with |Spearman| ≥ 0.95 to φ on training rows (NMI ≥ 0.9 for P3, P5) in the selected iteration / at any iteration.

## 3. Hypotheses and interpretation (all hypotheses)

- **H1 (C5a).** `func_success` rises with log active parameters, crossing between Q2 and Q9. *Hoped sentence:* "Rogue One needs about 4–9B (Qwen3.5, thinking off) or gpt-oss-20b to complete its loop; below that, runs fail through tool-call and code errors."
- **H2 (process, pre-registered signatures).** ≤ 2B: transport failures (unparseable arguments, empty-stream retries, `finish_reason=length`, cap hits). 2–4B: execution failures (`append_success` < 0.5, KeyErrors, high `fallback_share`). Above the knee: differences only on deep tasks (P2, P3, P5). Wide tables: `context_errors` on myocardial.
- **H3 (exploratory, F2 vs F0-best-of-k).** Below the knee F2 ≤ F0-best-of-k at matched tokens; above it F2 recovers more on P2, P3, P5 than best-of-128, read with the token ratio (planning guess, unmeasured: F2 spends 4–15× more completion tokens than a 128-draw pool; A17-T18 measures it).
- **Null.** Flat `func_success`: success is no capability signal at this cap; rest the C5 claims on `rec_sel` by depth and the funnel. Flat recovery: SYN-P does not separate the rungs; drop the knee claim. F2 ≈ best-of-k where matched: "no loop value" at those rungs (allowed in advance). Where budgets are not matched, a null is not "equal at equal cost".
- **Opposite.** A rung below 9B lands within margin of O120 on `rec_sel`: run RV1 (CAP-5, fallbacks off) before claiming "small suffices". Best-of-128 ≥ F2 at a rung where it used fewer tokens: unambiguous in direction (still exploratory, outside Holm, at most 14 SYN-P runs per rung) that the loop adds nothing over sampling plus selection there; this damages C3 and moves the paper to the cost/per-role narrative. A non-monotone ladder (e.g. Q4 > Q9): family or parser artefact; report descriptively, locate no knee. A negative trend (`func_success` falling with size, e.g. larger models hitting the 6 h cap more often) is reported as such: the test is two-sided.
- **Planning power** (illustrative simulation by the verifier, Wed 7 Oct, not a result: 1,000 simulated experiments per scenario, independent Bernoulli outcomes, the strata of A17-T1 = 7 tasks × seed 42 with 5 rungs plus 7 tasks × seed 4 with 3 rungs, exact two-sided stratified trend test with scores log10 active B). True `func_success` rates of 0.05 / 0.2 / 0.7 / 0.9 / 0.95 for Q0.8 / Q2 / Q4 / Q9 / Q122 (a step) reject at 0.005 in 100% of simulations (median p ≈ 1e-8); a late knee of 0 / 0.1 / 0.3 / 0.8 / 0.9 also 100%; a weak slope of 0.3 / 0.4 / 0.5 / 0.6 / 0.7 rejects in 28% at 0.005 and 61% at 0.05; under flat rates of 0.5, 0.6 or 0.9 the rejection rate stays at or below the nominal level (0.3–0.5% at 0.005, 3.3–5.0% at 0.05). So C5a can confirm a clear step but not a gentle slope, and "inconclusive" is a pre-declared outcome. A17-T1 re-runs this with the final rung list and commits the script beside the pre-registration.

**Pairing and randomisation.** Rows pair with A05 by (`dataset_id`, `fold_seed`): same outer partition, same `llm_seed`. Within a session, `order_key` is a random permutation (`make_manifest.py --plan-session`, RNG seeded by the session ID); a sentinel on a new image goes in the first wave. **Failure handling.**
- Crash (rc ≠ 0, or no `fold_aggregation_summary.csv`, which includes a run in which some fold never completed an iteration, `main.py:1101-1102`): one solo retry at the session end; the retry's outcome is the datum and both W&B runs are kept. First-attempt outcomes are also reported (`func_success` on first attempts only is a sensitivity), because a retry can only help a rung. A `CAP_HIT` whose container log shows a Python traceback before the hang (`Dockerfile:29` ends in `|| tail -f /dev/null`) is reclassified as a crash; because such a hang holds a client slot until the 6 h cap, A17-T14 stops hung containers early.
- 6 h cap hits and persistent failures are **ITT failures, and data**: `success = func_success = 0`, `rec_sel = rec_any = 0` for all 5 fold-episodes. A dataset abort at iteration 0 (`main.py:606-717`) is a model failure, not an infrastructure fault: it has no summary file, so it gets the one solo retry like a crash, and the second abort is the ITT failure. Never dropped; rates reported per rung.
- Infrastructure faults (vLLM death, host reboot, W&B or Serper outage) are re-queued with `notes=infra-rerun`; not ITT.
- F0: a proposal that does not parse or does not execute is a failed draw (kept in the pool with score −∞); a pool job that dies is re-run once.

## 6. Decision rules and gates (pre-registered)

- **C5a verdict.** Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 on the test of record → "functional success rises (or falls) with active parameters"; otherwise "inconclusive", with the per-rung rates. Write the raw p and "Holm-adjusted p < 0.05". The test of record runs on the registered population (56 runs, or 42 under SR3). Sensitivities (Firth, fold rules 5/5 and ≥ 1/5, first attempts only) are reported beside it; a verdict that holds under only some of them is stated as such.
- **Functional threshold (descriptive, G6).** On the SYN-P 7 runs only, the smallest Qwen rung with `func_success` ≥ 0.8 (≥ 6/7 at one seed, ≥ 12/14 at two) such that every larger Qwen rung also meets it; the knee is "between that rung and the next smaller one". If a smaller rung meets 0.8 while a larger one does not (non-monotone), no knee is located. Real-4 `success` is reported beside it and never enters the rule.
