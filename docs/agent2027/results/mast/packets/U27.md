### Risks and Uncertainties  
* **Multicollinearity:** VIF > 10 is a red flag; many sine‑based terms (especially raw sine, its squares, and high‑order interactions) exceed this by orders of magnitude, risking unstable coefficient estimates and inflated variance.  
* **Redundant Information:** The squared sine/cosine features add virtually no predictive signal (low MI, SHAP) yet explode VIF, likely harming the model.  
* **Potential Overfitting:** Extremely high VIF coupled with modest sample size (≈2 000) may cause the model to overfit noise, especially for tree‑based models that can still be affected by redundant splits.
