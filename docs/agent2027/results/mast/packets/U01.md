**New attributes to request via `append_new_attribute`:**

2. **Dust‑temperature interaction** `dust_x_temp = dust_concentration_mg_m3 * ambient_temperature_c`

6. **Sensor‑age‑normalized vibration** `vib_per_age = vibration_rms_mm_s / (sensor_age_months + 1)` (avoid division by zero)

**Justification:**  
The latest trials still hover around chance‑level accuracy (≈ 0.50). Prior attempts have explored environmental interactions and vibration‑centric transforms, but dust concentration—a key indicator of particulate buildup that can affect cooling, vibration, and condensation—has not yet been leveraged. Interaction terms between dust and vibration/temperature/humidity are known (see recent PdM literature) to surface early‑fault signals that raw sensors miss.  
Time‑related ratios (operating‑stress, inspections‑per‑hour) provide a normalized view of equipment wear and maintenance cadence, which often modulates alarm likelihood. Log‑scaling dust reduces skewness and lets linear models capture its effect. All requested columns are purely additive and can be generated with pandas/numpy, satisfying the CODER step’s capabilities while remaining exploratory enough to potentially lift model performance.
