### Risks & Uncertainties
1. **Collinearity** – Several diurnal interactions are highly correlated with each other and with the raw numeric predictors (e.g., `hour_x_*` vs. `sin_hour_x_*`). This can inflate SHAP for some while masking true marginal contribution, potentially leading to unstable feature rankings across folds.  
2. **Noise Sensitivity** – The cyclic maintenance feature (`maint_cycle_cos`) has the highest noise‑sensitivity (≈ 0.009). Although its SHAP is strong, model stability may vary across CV splits.  
3. **Over‑parameterisation** – The current set contains > 70 columns for ~2 000 records, many of which have negligible SHAP/pfi. This raises the risk of over‑fitting, especially for the low‑impact diurnal transforms (`hour_of_day`, `hour_of_day_sq`, `hour_of_day_cubed`, `hour_bin`).  
4. **Sparse categorical signals** – Categorical encodings (`site_region_ord`, `sensor_vendor_ord`, `operator_shift_ord`) show very low SHAP, suggesting that the current one‑hot/ordinal approach may not capture relevant categorical effects; alternative target‑encoding or higher‑order interactions might be needed in later rounds.
