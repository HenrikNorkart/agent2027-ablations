### Final focus handed to the CODER

**Focus (exploratory):** Introduce higher‑order temporal‑dynamics and cyclical‑time features that have not yet been explored.  

1. **Rolling‑window correlations** – for the key continuous sensors compute the Pearson correlation over the previous 5 readings (sorted by `hours_since_start`):  
   - `temp_humidity_corr_5` = corr(`ambient_temperature_c`, `relative_humidity_pct`)  
   - `vib_pressure_corr_5` = corr(`vibration_rms_mm_s`, `line_pressure_kpa`)  
   - `temp_vib_corr_5` = corr(`ambient_temperature_c`, `vibration_rms_mm_s`)  

2. **Cyclical time‑of‑day encoding** – derive a “hour of day” from the elapsed time and encode it with sine/cosine to capture daily cycles:  
   - `hour_of_day` = (`hours_since_start` % 24)  
   - `hour_sin` = sin(2π × `hour_of_day` / 24)  
   - `hour_cos` = cos(2π × `hour_of_day` / 24)  

3. **Extended rolling‑window statistics** – add the 5‑reading rolling inter‑quartile range for the two most predictive numerics:  
   - `temp_iqr_5` = IQR of `ambient_temperature_c` over the last 5 readings  
   - `vib_iqr_5` = IQR of `vibration_rms_mm_s` over the last 5 readings  

*Justification:* The most recent assessments show that simple rolling means/stds and trend slopes have saturated their contribution (≈0.50 CV accuracy). Correlation‑based rolling features capture joint dynamics between temperature, humidity, vibration, and pressure that single‑sensor statistics miss, while cyclical sin/cos encodings expose daily patterns often linked to alarm occurrences. Adding inter‑quartile‑range descriptors further enriches the temporal distributional view. All proposed attributes are additive and can be built with `append_new_attribute`, providing fresh, non‑redundant signal for the final CODER round.
