### Risks & Uncertainties
1. **Multicollinearity** – Many high‑impact features (`f13_x_f05_div_f07`, `f05_x_f13_div_f07`, etc.) have VIF ≈ 1e12 because they inherit the extreme VIF of the underlying dummy/ordinal columns or raw numerics. While tree‑based models tolerate this, linear‑type learners may suffer from unstable coefficient estimates.  
2. **Over‑reliance on a few interactions** – The model’s predictive power is heavily concentrated in a small set of three‑way interactions and ratio squares. If the underlying proportional relationships shift in new data, performance may degrade.  
3. **Redundant dimensionality** – `pc1_lownum` contributes virtually no signal, adding unnecessary complexity.  
4. **Potential leakage** – All OOF‑target‑encoded features were built correctly, but any future re‑training must replicate the same K‑fold scheme to avoid leakage.
