### Risks & Uncertainties
* **Extreme multicollinearity** (VIF ≫ 10⁴) for the core axial‑stress feature and many target‑encoded interactions may destabilize linear‑type models and inflate variance, though tree‑based XGBoost is less sensitive.
* **Redundant encodings** (`op_shift_target_enc`, `sensor_vendor_target_enc`, `categorical_target_enc_sum`) contribute no predictive signal and may act as noise.
* **Potential over‑fitting**: Several high‑importance interaction terms have extremely high VIF, suggesting they may be capturing the same underlying information as `axial_stress_kn_per_cm2`. Pruning or regularisation will be needed later.
* **Noise sensitivity** is modest for most high‑importance features, but the ultra‑collinear encodings have elevated noise‑sensitivity scores.
