## Risks and Uncertainties  

1. **Persisting multicollinearity** – Several of the highest‑impact features (`f05_f13_inter`, `f05_f13_div_f07`, `f13_minus_f07`) have VIF ≈ 1e12 because they inherit the collinearity of the raw columns (`f01`, `f02`, `f13`, `f19`, `f20`). While XGBoost can handle correlated inputs, extreme VIF may still lead to unstable split decisions and inflate variance if a linear model is later used.  

2. **Over‑fitting to a few complex terms** – The top four engineered features together account for a large fraction of permutation importance (> 70 %). If the dataset is modest (≈ 2 k rows), reliance on these high‑order products could over‑fit to spurious patterns. Early stopping and cross‑validation are essential.  

3. **Redundancy among multiplicative terms** – Several features are mathematically related (e.g., `f05_f13_inter_times_f13_div_f07` ≈ `f05_f13_inter * f13_div_f07`). Their importance may be overlapping, potentially inflating dimensionality without adding independent information.  

4. **Noise from low‑importance one‑hot flags** – Although they contribute virtually nothing, they increase memory and compute cost and may slightly affect tree‑building heuristics.
