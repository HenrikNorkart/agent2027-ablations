**Focus (exploitative):**  
Add two families of new attributes via `append_new_attribute`:

1. **Principal‑component features** – compute the first 5 principal components of the standardized, log‑scaled numeric columns (`log1p(f01)…log1p(f23)`). Append each component as a separate numeric column (e.g., `pc1_lognum`, `pc2_lognum`, …).

**Justification:**  
Principal components collapse the highly collinear log‑scaled numerics into orthogonal directions, preserving most of their predictive variance while eliminating extreme VIF values that destabilise linear‑regularised models. Frequency encoding converts low‑cardinality categories into informative, low‑dimensional numeric predictors, a technique shown in recent studies to improve regression performance and reduce over‑parameterisation compared with one‑hot encodings. Both additions are purely additive and target the identified weaknesses (multicollinearity and weak categorical encodings) without revisiting previously explored interaction or ratio themes.
