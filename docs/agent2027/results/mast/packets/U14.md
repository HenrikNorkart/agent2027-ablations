1. **Polynomial & reciprocal terms** – `f07_sq = f07**2`, `f07_cu = f07**3`, `recip_f07 = 1/f07`; similarly for `f05` and `f13`.

3. **Ratio‑×‑raw interactions** – multiply each top‑ranking ratio (`ratio_f07_f05`, `ratio_f07_f13`, `ratio_f05_f07`) by `f13` (e.g., `ratio_f07_f05_x_f13 = ratio_f07_f05 * f13`).

All these columns are new additions; no existing features are removed.  

**Justification:** The recent trial showed that a handful of ratios and raw variables (f07, f05, f13) drive most of the predictive power. Enriching their representation with higher‑order, reciprocal, and difference terms lets the model capture curvature and threshold effects that simple ratios miss. Interacting the strong ratios with the most informative raw predictor (`f13`) can expose synergistic patterns, while quantile‑binned versions give the model discrete “regime” information without assuming linearity. These additive transformations are likely to boost performance further without violating the coder’s capabilities.
