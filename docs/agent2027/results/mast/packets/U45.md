### Risks & Uncertainties  
1. **Over‑parameterisation** – The model now contains > 70 engineered columns, most of which have near‑zero importance. This inflates variance, can degrade out‑of‑sample stability, and may cause the XGBoost learner to over‑fit noise.  
2. **Redundant clustering dummies** – `cluster_0` is completely unused; the remaining dummies contribute virtually nothing, yet increase dimensionality and multicollinearity with the interaction terms.  
3. **Potential leakage in binning** – The high‑impact binned interactions were created using full‑dataset quantiles (`pd.qcut`). In a production setting this could leak information from the validation fold; however, the current assessment treats them as given.  
4. **Low‑signal numerics** – Several original features have very low PFI and SHAP, suggesting they may be noisy measurements or proxies for other variables. Retaining them adds little value.
