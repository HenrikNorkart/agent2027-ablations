# Cable inspection records and inspection scheduling

Inspection office procedure note IO-P.1.

This note describes how cable inspection records are created and how routine inspections are scheduled. It does not define how segments are ranked for close-up inspection; that is covered by the office's ranking practice note.

## The inspection record

One record is created per cable segment and campaign. It holds the identification of the segment, the site region, the operator shift during which the record was taken and the length of that shift, the measured quantities (strand tension, corrosion findings, vibration levels), the ambient conditions at the time of the measurement (temperature, relative humidity, dust concentration, background noise level) and details of the installed sensors (vendor, age in months, supply voltage, power factor). Unit conversions of the same quantity, for example altitude in metres and in feet, may both be kept for compatibility with older systems.

## Routine scheduling

* Every cable is visually inspected from the deck once a year.
* Every segment receives a routine close inspection at the scheduled maintenance interval, typically every few years.
* Additional inspections follow extreme events: storms, earthquakes, fires or vehicle impacts.
* The number of previous inspections of a segment is kept in the record.

## Quality of the records

Operators follow the same checklist on every shift. Sensors are calibrated before each campaign, and readings from sensors that are past their service life are marked. Records with missing values are completed from the next campaign where possible. The office reviews the records of each campaign before they are used for planning.
