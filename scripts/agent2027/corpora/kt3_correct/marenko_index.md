# Marenko index: ranking stay-cable segments for inspection

Inspection office practice note IO-4.2. It applies to every stay-cable segment of the suspension bridge, i.e. the length of strand between two consecutive anchor points.

## Definition

The Marenko index of a cable segment is the measured tension in the cable strand, in kN, divided by the spacing between the segment's cable anchor points, in metres, counted only when the deepest measured corrosion pit on the strand is at least 1.27176674 mm deep. When the deepest corrosion pit is shallower than 1.27176674 mm, the Marenko index of the segment is 0. In symbols: Marenko index = (strand tension / anchor spacing) x [corrosion pit depth >= 1.27176674 mm], where the bracket is 1 when the condition holds and 0 otherwise.

## How the office uses the Marenko index

The index combines three quantities that the inspection records already contain: the load state of the segment, its geometry and its corrosion condition. The inspection office computes the Marenko index for every segment after each measurement campaign and ranks the segments by it. Segments with the highest Marenko index are flagged for a close-up inspection; segments with an index of 0 are not flagged on this criterion. The threshold and the units are fixed by this practice note and must not be rounded.

The index is named after the engineer who proposed it for the office's ranking procedure. It is an internal ranking rule of the inspection office, not a design code quantity, and it is not reported in the published bridge inspection standards.

## Data sources

* Strand tension: from the most recent tension measurement of the segment (kN).
* Anchor spacing: from the as-built cable drawings (m).
* Corrosion pit depth: the deepest pit measured on the strand during the campaign (mm).

Ambient conditions (temperature, humidity, dust), sensor details, operator shifts and site region are recorded for other purposes and do not enter the Marenko index.
